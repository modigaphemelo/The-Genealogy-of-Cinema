From slavery narratives to contemporary racial justice cinema—each film examined through Fanon's decolonization, Du Bois' double consciousness, hooks' oppositional gaze, Spillers' grammar of flesh, Morrison's rememory, West's prophetic critique, and Baldwin's witness.
