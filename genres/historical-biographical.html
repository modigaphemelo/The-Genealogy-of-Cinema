Historical & Biographical

The past as contested terrain of power, memory, and identity.

Historical cinema as political memory—analyzing how power writes history through lenses of critical theory. Each film rated for historical consciousness and examined for its implicit theories of sovereignty, resistance, memory, trauma, and the construction of historical truth.
