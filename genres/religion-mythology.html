Religion & Mythology

Divine narratives analyzed through atheist-materialist frameworks.

Methodological Stance: Atheist-Materialist Analysis

This genre is analyzed from an explicit atheist, anti-theist, and materialist perspective. All religious claims are treated as human cultural productions to be examined genealogically rather than theological truths. Films are evaluated based on:

  • Intellectual honesty over emotional manipulation
  • Critical distance from religious dogma
  • Suspicion of institutional power in religious hierarchies
  • Humanist ethics without supernatural justifications
  • Genealogical analysis of religious concepts as social constructs

Higher ratings indicate films that successfully interrogate religious power structures, maintain critical distance, or offer philosophical complexity. Lower ratings indicate films that proselytize, manipulate emotionally, or avoid intellectual rigor.
